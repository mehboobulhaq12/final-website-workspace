    What is an AI operating system?

    An AI operating system is a production layer that combines workflow logic, routing, channel orchestration, integrations, dashboards, and human oversight to run business processes reliably.

    How is an AI operating system different from a chatbot?

    A chatbot is usually one interface. An AI operating system coordinates multiple workflows, channels, integrations, routing rules, and reporting inside one operational layer.
